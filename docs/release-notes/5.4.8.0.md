## Fix

- Fixed a regression that could hide the Known Enchantment icon when Better Third Person Selection hid the vanilla rollover text. The icon uses the current crosshair snapshot and remains suppressed during BookMenu and BookMode.

## In-game verification

The icon appeared beside the BTPS 3D pickup prompt for an enchanted Iron Greatsword in first and third person on Skyrim 1.6.1170 with BTPS 0.8.9. The native rollover text was hidden, and each targeting check completed without opening or closing a menu.

The legacy ESP and light ESL packages are distributed separately on Nexus Mods.
